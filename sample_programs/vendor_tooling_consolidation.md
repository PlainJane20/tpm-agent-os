# Program Brief: Operational Tooling Consolidation

Over three years of hypergrowth, IT and Security independently stood up
tooling to cover gaps as they appeared. Nobody owns the full list end to end.

What we know:
- At least 5 SaaS vendors provide overlapping identity/endpoint-adjacent
  capability; two were purchased by different teams within the same quarter
  without either team knowing about the other's contract.
- 46 internal automation projects currently run on contractor-owned
  infrastructure that was never formally handed to an internal team.
- ~200 external users have standing access to internal systems through a
  vendor-managed bridge that was meant to be temporary 18 months ago.
- Finance is asking for a Q4 cost-optimization number before renewals lock in
  for another year.
- No one has audited whether the 46 contractor-owned projects are still
  business-critical, cosmetic, or dead.

Ask: figure out what should be cut, what should be insourced, and what's
actually load-bearing -- and whether this is worth a dedicated program or
should just be folded into existing planning.
